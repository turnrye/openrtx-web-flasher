## Start here

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Put it on your radio" icon="rocket">
    The [flasher](flash/) runs in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Pick your radio, pick a version, and it walks
    you through a backup, flashing mode and the flash itself.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Is my radio supported?" icon="list-format">
    [Radios the flasher supports](flash/radios/) says how each one is flashed and whether that was checked.
    The [development status](dev_status/) page lists what works on each once OpenRTX is on it. [What a backup
    keeps](flash/backups/) says what the flasher can and cannot save first.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Using it" icon="open-book">
    The [users' guide](user_guide/) covers the screens, the menus and M17. Each radio has its own [hardware
    page](hardware/hardware/).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Help out" icon="heart">
    Every page has an "Edit page" link at the bottom. [How to contribute](contribute/) explains it in plain
    words: no programming, no special tools.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
