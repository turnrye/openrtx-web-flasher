# Photo permissions

A working note: photos of OpenRTX that exist elsewhere and would suit the site, who to
ask, and a draft message for each. None of them has been asked yet, and none is on the
site. A photo goes up only with its owner's answer in writing (a reply in the issue
tracker, the chat or by mail is enough), with the credit line they ask for.

What to ask for: a licence, ideally CC BY-SA 4.0, the licence proposed for the website's
text in OpenRTX/openrtx.github.io#19. If an owner prefers not to, permission to show the
photo on openrtx.org with their credit is enough for that photo alone; note which.

  page. They are the project's, but the website states no licence: the same question as
  #19, for the project's owners.

## Draft messages

For a person in the OpenRTX chat:

> Hi! I'm redoing the OpenRTX home page and would love to use your photo of
> [what it shows] ([link]). Would you be OK with it going on openrtx.org under CC BY-SA
> 4.0, credited as [name and callsign]? If you'd rather keep a different licence, just
> permission to show it on the site with credit is fine too. Thanks!

For the M17 Project, about several photos at once:

> Hi! I'm working on a new openrtx.org with photos of radios running OpenRTX. Your posts
> have some of the best ones: [list with links]. Could we use them on the site under
> CC BY-SA 4.0, with the credit you'd like to see under each? For the Friedrichshafen
> photos, could you also check with the people in them? The DEF CON photo says it is
> courtesy of hacker.house, so if they are the ones to ask, a pointer would be great.
> Thanks!

For EURAO:

> Hello, I'm working on the OpenRTX project's website and would like to show your photo of
> the M17 team at HAM RADIO 2024 ([link]). May we use it, credited to EURAO, under
> CC BY-SA 4.0 or with your permission for the site only? Thank you.

## When an answer comes

1. Save the photo at full size, and the answer (a link, or a copy of the mail).
2. Note it here: the licence, the credit line, the date.
3. Add the photo to the website's `_media/` with its credit in the caption.
