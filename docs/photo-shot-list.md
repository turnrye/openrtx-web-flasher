# Photo shot list

A working note for whoever photographs radios for OpenRTX. The OpenRTX website's home
page (OpenRTX/openrtx.github.io), the radio cards there and in the flasher, and the
flasher's "flashing mode" step all show line drawings today; each photo below replaces
one of them, or fills a place the pages now leave for one.

The radios on the bench: MD-UV390, CS7000-M17 Plus, Retevis C62, MD-9600, RT-950 Pro,
T-TWR Plus. Other radios' photos come from their owners, through the suggestion form
(see [photo-permissions.md](photo-permissions.md)).

## How

- **Background:** matte black or very dark grey, edge to edge. The site is black; a grey
  sweep reads as a box on it.
- **Light:** one warm light from the side, a white card opposite to lift the shadows. No
  flash: it flattens the screen and makes reflections.
- **Screen:** OpenRTX running, brightness turned down until the screen is not blown out.
  Shoot a second frame for the screen alone if the radio's body and the screen will not
  both expose well.
- **Antenna:** fitted. No stickers, price tags or protective film.
- **Size:** at least 2400 px on the long side, JPEG, landscape 3:2 unless the shot says
  otherwise. The build makes the smaller copies.
- **Names:** `<radio>-<shot>.jpg` with the flasher's radio name, such as
  `mduv3x0-hero.jpg` or `cs7000p-screen.jpg`.
- **Licence:** decide it before the first photo goes up. The website's text licence is
  still open (OpenRTX/openrtx.github.io#19); CC BY-SA 4.0 matches that proposal.

## The shots for every radio

| Shot     | What                                                                                 | Where it goes                                   |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| `hero`   | Three-quarter view from the front left, screen on, the whole radio in frame.         | Home page hero, the radio's page                |
| `front`  | Straight on, centred, square crop, the radio filling about two thirds of the height. | Radio cards on the home page and in the flasher |
| `screen` | The screen alone, square on, no glare.                                               | Next to the screen captures                     |
| `hand`   | In a hand, outdoors or at a desk with a radio around: the radio in use.              | Home page, the radio's page                     |
| `enter`  | The keys held for flashing mode, fingers on them, the programming cable plugged in.  | The flasher's "flashing mode" step              |
| `cable`  | The programming cable's plug next to the radio's socket, before it goes in.          | The flasher's "before you start"                |
