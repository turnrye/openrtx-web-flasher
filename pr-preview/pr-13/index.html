Firmware that frees your radio

OpenRTX is community-built firmware for handhelds and mobiles from TYT, Connect Systems, Retevis and others. It is open source, and it brings M17 digital voice to the radio you already own.

M17 digital voice

Talk M17 on a radio that never had it

M17 receive and transmit work on the MD-UV380, the UHF MD-380, Module 17 and both CS7000-M17 models, and receive on the VHF MD-380. Some TYT radios need a small hardware mod first.

Firmware you can read, change and build

OpenRTX is GPL-3.0 and written in the open on GitHub. Every change is built by CI, and the flasher can put any of those builds on a radio, so trying a change takes minutes.
